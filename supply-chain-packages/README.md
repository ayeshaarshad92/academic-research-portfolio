# Supply Chain Analysis – Packages Convertors Limited

## Overview
Academic supply chain analysis of Packages Convertors Limited (PCL),
covering its supply chain strategy, suppliers, distribution,
manufacturing process and supply chain drivers.

## My Contribution
- Supply chain research and analysis
- Company and industry research
- Supply chain mapping
- Analysis of suppliers and raw materials
- Distribution analysis
- Manufacturing process analysis
- Recommendations development

## Key Areas
- Supply Chain Strategy
- Supply Chain Metrics & Drivers
- Supplier & Raw Material Analysis
- Distribution Modeling
- Manufacturing Process Mapping
- Generalized Supply Chain Model
- Pipeline Mapping
- Recommendations

## Research Method
Field visit and interview, supplemented by company reports and
publicly available information.

## Project Type
Academic Group Project – Fundamentals of Supply Chain Management
